Two of the four proposed new Skills are merged into the reference deltas for existing Skills instead of becoming separate Skills. The unique parts of the security-change-review proposal (trust-boundary scope, extra commonly missed patterns, evidence levels, review kept separate from repair) now live in the security-and-hardening references, and the unique parts of the incident-postmortem proposal (declare, stabilize, timeline, contributing causes, impact, actions, verified close, by kind of incident) are a new incident-handling reference of the debugging Skill that points to the runbook reference instead of repeating it. Both are staged as candidates; nothing is promoted or activated.
