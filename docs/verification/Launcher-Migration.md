## Method and limits

Replica `replica-recon` was adapted to inventory the user-supplied source and map prototype flows to existing owners; this is source-derived adaptation, with no verified public publisher/license claim. Addy `deprecation-and-migration` preserved stable routes/handlers and mapped unsupported behavior, `frontend-ui-engineering` supplied responsive/accessibility/state checks, and `browser-testing-with-devtools` was adapted to the available rendering tools. `debugging-and-error-recovery` supplied the resident reproduction, failing regression, one-line correction and successful replay. `mind-control` guided separation of measured evidence, scoped declarations and unknown data. `agent-core-runtime-activation-audit` supplies source/build/icon identity and post-restart checks. Exact-revision actual-use receipts were recorded for the seven selected native catalog workflows; comparative benefit remains unavailable and trust was not changed.

No new backend feature was requested or built. There were no dependency downloads/installations, new grants, commits, pushes or releases. Full backend/.NET suites, provider latency/performance benchmarks, manual assistive-technology testing and sending a new task through the migrated composer are outside the checks performed. CDP navigation/error observation covers actual resident pages; it does not prove every mutation workflow or a successful new model inference. The Native partner did not deliver a final review. Existing backend behavior is covered only by retained UI regressions and the resident checks recorded here; no blanket end-to-end claim is made.
